How it works

  1. DescribeType an animal. AI draws it and suggests a name, ticker and description. Redraw as often as you like.
  2. Make it yoursEdit the details and add your own links if you want them.
  3. LaunchYour wallet signs a few quick transactions: create the coin, set its fee split, and (if you want) make the first buy. It trades on a pump.fun bonding curve and its price rises as people buy.
  4. EarnCreators earn a share of the creator fees on every trade.

About

HatchPad is a test launcher for animal-mascot coins. It runs on Solana devnet, so every coin and every SOL here is play money. It exists to try the idea out before any real money is involved.

HatchPad launches animal-mascot coins on Solana. You describe an animal, AI draws it, and your wallet launches it on a bonding curve. Coins are created by users and can lose all their value.

Is this real money?

No. It runs on devnet, Solana's test network. Real-money mainnet is switched off in the code.

Is this real money?

Yes. Every buy, sell and launch uses real SOL. Only spend what you can afford to lose.

Who gets the creator fees?

Each coin splits its creator fees between the wallet that launched it and HatchPad (the exact split is shown on the coin page). Anyone can press Send out fees on the coin page; the money always goes to those two wallets.

What happens when the curve fills?

pump.fun moves the coin to its regular trading pool (PumpSwap). After that, trade it on pump.fun or any Solana DEX.

Where do the pictures come from?

AI makes them from your description, and asks for an original character. It refuses well-known characters, brands and people, but it is not perfect. Pictures of launched coins are kept; animals you do not launch are deleted after a day.

What does it cost?

Network fees, the small rent for the new accounts, and any buy you make. HatchPad's cut comes only from the creator fee on trades.
